Spoken cues

Hear it before
you see it.

Fifteen seconds out: “Left. Onto Dry Creek Road.” Then pips rise in pitch and quicken as the bar fills. A chime at the turn.

  • The voice is built in — nothing to install
  • Night map for the dark
  • Bluetooth helmets get it as navigation audio

Pre-run

Record it.
Stop. Tap CUE.

Ride the course once with the recorder on. At every turn and hazard, pick the arrow and say what it is. The line and the cues save as one GPX.

  • Keeps recording with the screen off
  • Notes, dangers in three levels, U-turns, keep left/right
  • Share the file with anyone
