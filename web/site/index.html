Roadbook

Ride the roadbook.
Never look down.

Offline turn-by-turn for motorcycles. An arrow that fills exactly to the turn, pips in your helmet, the cue read aloud. No signal, no account, no tracking.

Three things it does

Real map renders from the Montana pack. The manual walks through every screen.

Map packs

Whole states, downloaded inside the app: MAPS → DOWNLOAD, pick a region, then Roads and/or Terrain. After that, no signal needed.

Install

  1. On the phone, open roadbook.apk; allow installs from that app; Install.
  2. Allow Precise location. That's the only thing it asks for.
  3. MAPS → DOWNLOAD your states. Ride.

Updates: download again and install over it. Settings and packs stay. Works on GrapheneOS.
