Roadbookplanner

Click the map twice: a start and an end. The route follows the mode above.

Drag the line onto any road or trail to pull the route through it. Drag a point to move it; click a point to delete it. Drop END onto START, or press loop, to finish where you started. Stops along the way are lettered A, B, C.

Click the line to add a cue, inspect the spot, or add a stop.

Or : keep its track as recorded and edit the cues, or route it on roads.
