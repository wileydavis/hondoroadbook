Map coverage

Whole states and provinces — the US, Canada and Baja — downloaded inside the app: MAPS → DOWNLOAD, pick a region, then Roads and/or Terrain. After that, no signal needed. The files are here too, for sideloading or a look: a pack is a zip of the map tiles, the routing graph and a place index, built from OpenStreetMap and rebuilt as it changes.

Roads carry the map, the router and the places; Terrain is hillshade and contours under them. Sizes are per pack; a Western state is ½–1 GB of roads and ¼–½ GB of terrain.
