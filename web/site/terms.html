1. What Roadbook is

Roadbook (the apps and the planner at hondoroadbook.com) plans routes and gives turn-by-turn cues from map data built by OpenStreetMap contributors and from terrain data published by others. It is offered free of charge, as is, by Hondo Garage ("we"). Using it means you accept these terms.

2. The map can be wrong

Roads close, gates lock, bridges wash out, a track that is public on the map is posted private on the ground, a road marked paved is not, and a legal road may be unsafe for you, your vehicle or the conditions. Roadbook does not know any of this. A route is a suggestion computed from imperfect data; a cue is a reminder of what the data says is there. Nothing in Roadbook is a statement that a road exists, is open, is legal to use, or is safe.

3. You are in charge

You are responsible for operating your vehicle safely and legally at all times: for watching the road rather than the screen, for obeying signs, closures, speed limits and private-property postings, for judging whether a road is within your ability and your vehicle's, and for carrying what you need where there is no help. Mount the phone so you can glance at it; set routes up before you move; use the voice cues so you do not have to look. If a cue and the road disagree, the road wins.
